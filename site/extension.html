02 —INSTALL (3 MIN)

1

download the extension

click this link and grab grugs-rug-radar-v1.4.1.zip from the latest release. unzip it — you'll get a folder called extension.

2

open chrome extensions page

paste chrome://extensions into your address bar. works on brave, edge, arc, vivaldi too — same page.

3

turn on developer mode

flip the Developer mode switch in the top-right corner. new buttons appear.

4

load unpacked

click Load unpacked and pick the extension folder you unzipped in step 1. done — you'll see grug's icon in your toolbar.

03 —UNLOCK (1 MIN)

1

go to the unlock page

click grug's icon in the toolbar → hit GET UNLOCK CODE, or go straight to grugnft.xyz/extension-unlock.

2

connect wallet + sign

connect the wallet holding your grugs (10+ on robinhood chain). grug checks your balance, then asks for one signature — no gas, no transaction.

3

paste the code

copy the unlock code the site gives you → open the extension popup → paste into the unlock field → hit unlock. badge activates for 7 days across all opensea pages.

◆ SAFETY ◆

the extension never touches your wallet. it can't sign transactions, can't move funds, can't read your balance from inside the popup. the only wallet signature is the one-time unlock on grugnft.xyz — outside the extension entirely.

no data collection. everything runs locally against public rpcs and the read-only scanner api. no analytics, no tracking, no accounts.

open source. the entire extension is in this repo — read every line before you install.
